Employee(Emp_ID, Emp_Name, Department, Salary, Joining_Date, DOB, Last_Login)

1.Employees joined in a particular year
Find all employees who joined the company in 2023. Display employee name, department, and joining date.

2.Employees joined in the last 6 months
Find employees whose Joining_Date falls within the last 6 months from today. Display their name and joining date, sorted by joining date from newest to oldest.

3.Calculate employee experience
Display each employee's name, joining date, and their experience in years as of today. Show employees having more than 3 years of experience.

4.Employees with upcoming birthdays
Find employees whose birthday falls in the current month. Display employee name, DOB, and department.

5.Department-wise average experience
Find the average employee experience in years for each department. Display only those departments where the average experience is more than 5 years.

6.Monthly joining analysis
Find the number of employees who joined in each year and month. Display Joining_Year, Joining_Month, and Employee_Count, sorted chronologically.

7.Inactive employees
Find employees whose Last_Login was more than 30 days ago. Display their name, last login date, and the number of days since their last login.

8.Employees joined on weekends
Find employees who joined on Saturday or Sunday. Display employee name, joining date, and the day of the week on which they joined.

9.Department-wise recent joiners
For each department, find the number of employees who joined during 2025. Display only departments having at least 5 employees who joined in that year.
Birthday age analysis

10.Calculate the current age of every employee using DOB. Display employee name, DOB, and age. Then display only employees whose age is between 25 and 35 years, ordered by age from highest to lowest.
